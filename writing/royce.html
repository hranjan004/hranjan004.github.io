Rereading Winston Royce

A look beyond the waterfall shorthand at feedback, prototyping and the management of software risk.

An overview of three related Royce essays, consolidated from The Complete Record rather than counted as separate works.

I returned to Royce’s 1970 paper to examine what gets lost when a systems-engineering argument becomes a simple lifecycle diagram. The writing follows feedback, staged learning and the need to discover problems before they become expensive.

The same questions surfaced in our Slug Board retrospective: how can user stories and integration work make developers communicate earlier, and how can process make a team’s assumptions visible?
