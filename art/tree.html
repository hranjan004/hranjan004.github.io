The Tree and Three Acts

Wooden sticks, stones and soil become a scene about growth and the passage of time. I arranged simple materials within a landscape and used photography to explore how a small gesture can carry a larger narrative.

Artist statement

My work begins with observation and the passage of time. Through photography and deliberate placement of wooden sticks, stones, and soil, I developed a scene that is grounded and contemplative. Three sticks rise from the earth against a scenic backdrop, each one standing in for an act in the long arc of growing up. The form explores transformation and the quiet accumulation of experience, interacting with space by borrowing from the landscape rather than competing with it. Across this project I became interested in how the simplest natural materials can carry the weight of an entire life's narrative when arranged with care. The relationship between nature and personal becoming is something worth sitting with.
