The Crown that Carries Memory

A Technoblade-inspired crown built from cardboard, paper, wire and reused ramen packaging. Its deliberately rectangular shape carries a digital memory into a physical object; the packaging provides both reinforcement and a visible record of resourcefulness.

Original artwork and photograph by Harsh Ranjan. Image reproduced from the supplied project and writing archive.
