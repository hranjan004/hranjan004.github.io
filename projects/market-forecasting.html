The problem

Financial observations can shift over time and contain heavy noise. A forecasting project must define a credible evaluation before treating a fitted pattern as useful signal.

How I approached it

I contributed to the project framing and presentation around a boosting baseline, feature selection and a possible LSTM follow-up.

The planned comparison separates tabular feature interactions from sequence effects and calls for evaluation on data outside the fitting period.

What changed

Developed the technical approach, project schedule and presentation materials.

The next evidence needed is a reproducible training run and out-of-period validation.

The export documents planning and presentation work. It does not substantiate a leaderboard score, successful backtest or trading return.

Engineering decisions

Chronological validation matters because random splits can mix future information into training. A complex sequence model needs to justify its cost against the baseline on the same evaluation period.
