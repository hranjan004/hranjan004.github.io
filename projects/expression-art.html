The problem

A procedural image can be represented as an expression evaluated at coordinates. The representation needs to support composition while remaining easy to inspect and traverse.

How I approached it

I worked with an Expr type containing coordinate variables, sine, cosine, averaging, multiplication and threshold branches.

I implemented recursive depth calculation and constructed a compound expression combining the two coordinates with trigonometric functions.

What changed

Built the expression representation and depth-traversal exercise.

The result makes a visual formula inspectable as a tree of typed operations.

Completed refers to the expression-tree exercise. The archive does not establish a finished generative-art application or gallery.

Engineering decisions

Algebraic data types make each operation explicit. A structural recursion handles leaves directly and derives a parent’s depth from the deepest child.
