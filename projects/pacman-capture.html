The opportunity

Two agents must collect food, protect home territory and avoid interfering with each other. A fixed offensive or defensive role cannot respond to every board state.

What I engineered

I developed weighted features for food, capsules, visible opponents, teammate distance and returning home, with attack/defense switching as the board changes.

I tuned stop/reverse penalties, corridor handling and trap-avoidance behavior using maze-distance features.

The results

Implemented a cooperative team policy and documented its representation, decision rules and evaluation limits.

Why it matters

A useful team policy makes competing goals explicit: collect rewards, protect territory and coordinate movement.

Engineering decisions & measurement context

Maze distance captures walls that Manhattan distance ignores. Feature weights still need care: rewarding distance from a teammate or opponent too strongly can overpower the goal of making progress.
