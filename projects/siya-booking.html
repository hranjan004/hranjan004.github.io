The problem

Customers who already know their trip should be able to start a booking without navigating a crowded landing page.

How I approached it

I scoped the information needed for a quick cab booking and explored desktop layouts around that primary task.

I iterated on visual density, separating essential booking fields from destination inspiration and supporting information.

What changed

Developed a booking-form direction and landing-page design concepts.

The study clarified the shortest useful path from trip intent to a booking request.

This is a design study. A deployed booking backend, completed bookings and conversion uplift are not established.

Engineering decisions

The core interface question is which fields are required to price or schedule a trip and which can wait. Progressive disclosure reduces the initial decision load without losing necessary details.
