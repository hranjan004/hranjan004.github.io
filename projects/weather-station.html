The problem

A small connected device needs to obtain configuration, gather sensor data and report readings to a service reliably.

How I approached it

The ESP32-C3 fetches the server’s location, queries an outdoor-weather service and reads its onboard sensor.

The Raspberry Pi serves location data and accepts posted readings, separating device acquisition from server-side logging.

What changed

Implemented the coursework device/server workflow for the device/server workflow; embedded coursework is ongoing.

What I took from it

The device/server boundary is the central challenge: configuration, HTTP requests and returned weather data need a clear contract. I treat the onboard sensor as a device reading rather than a calibrated ambient-temperature measurement.

Engineering decisions

The device obtains location from GET /location and posts its readings to the Raspberry Pi service. Separating configuration, acquisition and reporting makes the device/server protocol explicit.
