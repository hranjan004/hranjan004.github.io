The opportunity

Appliance-level measurements can strengthen Microgrid Manager’s panel-level estimates. I first benchmarked acquisition and arithmetic on the ESP32-C3, then specified the isolated sensing and telemetry design.

What I engineered

Wrote ESP-IDF C benchmarks for two-channel continuous ADC acquisition, blocking reads, LEDC loopback timing and fixed-point arithmetic.

Designed an isolated 9 V AC transformer front end with a biased divider. The power-meter proposal adds an SCT-013-030 current clamp.

Specified one JSON update per second over Wi-Fi to a Raspberry Pi, then Kafka forwarding using the eGauge field layout. MQTT publication to Home Assistant is a proposed extension.

The results

Sustained 3,846 samples/s per channel with no dropped or foreign samples during a two-second continuous-ADC run.

Measured 16,666.40 µs mean period and 0.49 µs standard deviation across 1,289 periods of a 60 Hz LEDC loopback signal, with Wi-Fi off.

Fixed-point multiply-accumulate took 38.4 cycles/sample versus 673.1 for software float—a 17.5× improvement. A blocking ADC read took 48.6 µs.

Why it matters

Continuous acquisition and fixed-point arithmetic give this small processor a practical processing budget. The next build stages are the isolated front end, reference-meter calibration and Pi telemetry.

Engineering decisions & measurement context

The board is an ESP32-C3-DevKit-RUST with RUST-2 I2C wiring; firmware is flashed from the Pi over USB. C is the current implementation language; a Rust sampling-loop port is a stretch goal.

Targets, not results: real power within 3% of a reference meter above 100 W; frequency agreement within 1 mHz; voltage sag/swell detection within one cycle. Proposed validation loads are a kettle and a fan or laptop charger, using a Kill A Watt P4400 reference.

Home Assistant was absent from the original proposals. Adding MQTT to the custom ESP-IDF firmware is the proposed integration path. No Pi deployment or newer hardware work has been verified.

Completed work consists of two proposals and benchmark firmware. The voltage transformer and current clamp have not been bought or wired; appliance voltage, power accuracy and end-to-end telemetry latency have not been measured.
