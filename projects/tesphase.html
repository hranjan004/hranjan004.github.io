The problem

Solar homeowners needed an approachable way to coordinate their energy use. At Tesphase, my role as CTO was to connect customer needs with a practical product roadmap and working application.

How I approached it

I used more than 30 customer interviews to understand the workflows worth building first, then translated those findings into engineering priorities.

React Native provided the mobile interface, Supabase supported authentication and real-time data, and Node.js supported backend workflows. I owned the roadmap and architecture.

What changed

Shipped authentication, real-time database synchronization and push notifications.

Tesphase won SC Launchpad 2025. My CTO role ran from April through September 2025.

What I took from it

As CTO, I connected product priorities to implementation: authentication, synchronization and notifications had to support a coherent customer experience. Early savings estimates remained product hypotheses to test with customers.

Engineering decisions

Authentication establishes the user identity used by application data. Real-time synchronization and push notifications serve different paths: updating shared state in the app versus drawing attention to an event. Product interviews informed which workflows received engineering effort first.
