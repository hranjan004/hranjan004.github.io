The opportunity

Solar homeowners needed an approachable way to coordinate their energy use. At Tesphase, my role as CTO was to connect customer needs with a practical product roadmap and working application.

What I engineered

I translated more than 30 NSF I-Corps customer interviews into product priorities and owned the engineering roadmap.

I led a React Native mobile architecture with Supabase authentication and real-time data, plus Node.js backend workflows.

The results

Delivered authentication, real-time database synchronization and push notifications.

Won SC Launchpad 2025. I served as CTO from April through September 2025.

Why it matters

Customer discovery gave the roadmap its order. Architecture and delivery turned those priorities into working product features.

Engineering decisions & measurement context

Authentication establishes the user identity used by application data. Real-time synchronization and push notifications serve different paths: updating shared state in the app versus drawing attention to an event. Product interviews informed which workflows received engineering effort first.

Early energy-savings estimates were product hypotheses for customer validation, not measured savings.
