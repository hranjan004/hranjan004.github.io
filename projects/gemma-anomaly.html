The problem

Individual electrical panels can produce related anomaly signals. The aim was to turn those signals into one reviewable incident without changing the existing appliance-inference service.

How I approached it

I built three logical panel agents around one Gemma adapter conditioned on panel identity. Their outputs are coordinated into a shared incident for review.

QLoRA with Hugging Face PEFT and TRL supported fine-tuning on Lambda GPUs; vLLM served the model. Respan captured traces and Nango connected a Google Sheets review workflow.

With only two verification rows in the anomaly table, I bootstrapped training labels by replaying the rule engine over archived readings.

What changed

Completed a hackathon prototype with model serving, traceability and a human review path.

Kept the anomaly layer separate from the BiLSTM / ONNX production inference path.

Completed refers to the prototype. Independent anomaly-detection accuracy and production alert outcomes are not established.

Engineering decisions

Panel identity conditions a shared adapter rather than requiring three separately fine-tuned models. The incident coordinator combines those panel-level outputs into one reviewable event.

QLoRA reduces the fine-tuning memory requirement; vLLM handles serving. Respan traces and the Sheets review workflow expose model behavior, but do not by themselves establish anomaly accuracy.
